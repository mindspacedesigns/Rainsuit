Questions we hear most

What is the difference between SEO and SEM?

SEO improves your unpaid, organic rankings in search results.

How long does SEO take to show results?

Most websites see meaningful movement in 3 to 6 months, and competitive keywords can take longer. Results depend on competition, site quality and consistent effort.

Can anyone guarantee a number one ranking on Google?

No. Google's rankings depend on many factors outside any agency's control, so guaranteed rankings are a warning sign.

What is local SEO and why does it matter?

Local SEO helps a business appear in searches tied to a location, such as near me queries and Google Maps. A complete Google Business Profile, consistent contact details and reviews are key factors.

What are on-page, off-page and technical SEO?

On-page SEO covers content and page elements, off-page SEO covers external signals such as backlinks, and technical SEO covers site speed, crawlability, indexing and structured data.

How does your technical SEO ensure visibility across Bangalore hubs?

It goes beyond basic meta tags. We implement Schema.org JSON-LD graphs (Organization, LocalBusiness, ProfessionalService, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList), automated XML sitemaps submitted to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, canonical URL management and mobile-friendly semantic headings that help search bots and AI answer engines index and cite your business accurately.
