Q. What factors affect the cost of building a website? A. Website development costs depend on the number of pages, design complexity, features, functionality, integrations, and maintenance requirements. E-commerce websites and custom web applications may cost more than basic business websites because they require additional development and features. Q. How long does it take to build a website? A. A standard business website usually takes 2 to 4 weeks. E-commerce stores and custom web applications typically take 5 to 8 weeks or more, depending on features and how quickly content and feedback are provided. Q. Should I hire a freelancer or a web design agency? A. Freelancers can suit small, fixed-scope projects at a lower cost. Agencies generally provide a team covering design, development, SEO and ongoing support, which helps for larger or long-term projects. Whichever you choose, review past work, ask for references and agree the scope in writing. Q. What is the difference between a WordPress website and a custom-coded website? A. WordPress and other CMS platforms are faster and cheaper to launch and easy to update yourself. Custom-coded websites take longer and cost more but allow tailored features, integrations and tighter control over performance and security. Q. Who owns the website, domain and source code after the project? A. Ownership should be stated in your contract. The domain should be registered in your name, and the design files and source code are normally transferred to you after final payment. Confirm this in writing before the project starts. Q. How long does SEO take to show results? A. SEO is gradual. Most websites see early movement in 3 to 6 months, and the timeline depends on competition, the age of the site, content quality and technical health. Paid search such as Google Ads can bring traffic sooner. Q. Do I need hosting and maintenance after the website launches? A. Yes. A website needs hosting, an SSL certificate, regular backups, software updates and security monitoring. Some agencies include these in an annual maintenance plan, so check what is covered and what is charged separately. Q. What does a web design and development company do? A. It plans, designs, builds and maintains websites and web applications for businesses. Many also handle related work such as SEO, e-commerce, mobile apps, hosting support and ongoing updates. Q. What services does a full-service digital agency usually offer? A. Typical services include website design and development, e-commerce, software and app development, SEO and paid search, content creation and website maintenance. Offerings vary by agency, so confirm the exact scope before you sign. Q. What is the difference between a web agency and a freelancer? A. A freelancer is one person handling most tasks, while an agency has separate designers, developers and SEO or content specialists. Agencies suit larger or multi-skill projects, and freelancers can suit small, well-defined tasks. Q. What should I check before hiring a web development company? A. Review their past projects and live websites, confirm who owns the code and content, and check what the contract says about timelines, payment stages, revisions and post-launch support. Q. What are the usual stages of a website project? A. Most projects move through requirements gathering, design mockups, development, testing, launch and maintenance. Clear sign-off at each stage helps avoid delays and rework. Q. Do I own my website after it is built? A. Ownership depends on your agreement. Confirm in writing that you own the domain, design files, source code and content, and that you receive all admin logins at handover. Q. What does work culture mean in an IT company? A. Work culture is the set of values, communication habits and ways of working that shape daily life at a company, including how teams collaborate, give feedback, handle deadlines and support learning. Q. How can I judge a company's culture before joining? A. Talk to current or former employees, read reviews on career platforms, and check the company's website and social media for how it presents its team and values. If possible, visit the office or meet the team before accepting an offer. Q. What is work-life balance in a software job? A. It means having manageable workloads, predictable working hours and enough time off to rest outside work. Realistic planning and clear priorities are what make it possible. Q. What does a typical day look like for a web developer? A. A typical day includes a short team stand-up, writing and testing code, fixing bugs, reviewing teammates' work and discussing requirements with designers or project managers. Q. How do learning opportunities help early-career employees? A. Mentoring, code reviews and exposure to real projects help new employees build practical skills faster than self-study alone, and they build confidence in professional workflows. Q. What questions should I ask in a job interview about culture? A. Useful questions include how the team handles disagreements, how success is measured in the role, how feedback and promotions work, what onboarding looks like and what growth paths exist in the first year. Q. What should a good web design portfolio show? A. It should show live or well-documented projects, the type of business each was for, the problem being solved, the features built and the results where available. Variety across industries and project types is a plus. Q. How do I evaluate a web company's portfolio? A. Open the live sites on both mobile and desktop, check loading speed and navigation, and look for consistency in design quality. Ask which parts of each project the company personally built. Q. What is a case study in a portfolio? A. A case study explains a project in detail: the client's goal, the challenges, the approach taken and the outcome. It gives more insight than a screenshot alone. Q. Should I ask for client references? A. Yes. Speaking with a past client reveals how the team communicates, handles changes and delivers on time, which a portfolio cannot show. Q. How can I check if a portfolio website is fast and mobile-friendly? A. Test the URL with Google PageSpeed Insights and view it on a real phone. Look at Core Web Vitals, layout shifts and whether buttons and text are easy to use on small screens. Q. Why do portfolios of similar-looking websites differ in quality? A. Two sites can look alike but differ in code quality, speed, security, accessibility and SEO setup. Those factors affect long-term performance and are not obvious from screenshots. Q. What details should I share when asking for a website quote? A. Share your business type, the goal of the website, the number of pages, required features such as payments or booking, any reference sites, your timeline and an approximate budget range. More detail leads to a more accurate quote. Q. What is a project brief? A. A project brief is a short document describing your business, goals, target audience, features, design preferences, deadlines and budget. It gives the development team a clear starting point. Q. What is the difference between a quote and a proposal? A. A quote lists the price for a defined scope. A proposal is broader and usually includes the approach, deliverables, timeline, team, payment terms and the price. Q. What is a discovery call? A. A discovery call is an initial conversation where both sides discuss your needs, goals and constraints so the agency can recommend a suitable approach and estimate the work. Q. Should I sign an NDA before sharing my idea? A. If your project involves a confidential idea, process or data, asking for a non-disclosure agreement is reasonable and common practice before sharing details. Q. What materials should I keep ready before my first meeting? A. Have your logo, brand colours, existing website link if any, sample content, competitor or inspiration sites and a list of must-have features ready to speed up the discussion. Q. What skills do I need to start a career in web development? A. Start with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, then learn a front-end framework such as React and a back-end language such as Node.js or PHP. Basic knowledge of databases, Git and problem-solving is also expected. Q. What does a full-stack developer do? A. A full-stack developer works on both the front end that users see and the back end that handles data, logic and servers, including databases and APIs. Q. What does a UI/UX designer do? A. A UI/UX designer researches how users behave, plans user flows, and creates wireframes and visual designs so a product is easy and pleasant to use. Q. Do I need a portfolio when applying for a developer or designer role? A. A portfolio or GitHub profile helps a lot, especially for freshers. It shows real work such as projects, code samples or design case studies beyond what a resume can convey. Q. What should a developer resume include? A. List your technical skills, projects with the technologies used, work experience with measurable outcomes, education, and links to GitHub or a portfolio. Keep it to one or two pages. Q. How should I prepare for a technical interview? A. Revise core concepts of the language and framework you use, practise common coding problems, review your own projects so you can explain your decisions, and prepare questions to ask the interviewer. Q. What does a PHP or Node.js software engineer do? A. They build the server-side logic of websites and applications, including APIs, database operations, authentication and integrations, and they fix bugs and improve performance. Q. What is the difference between PHP and Node.js? A. PHP is a server-side language widely used for websites and CMS platforms like WordPress. Node.js lets developers run JavaScript on the server and is often used for real-time and API-driven applications. Q. What does an SEO specialist do? A. An SEO specialist researches keywords, optimises page content and structure, fixes technical issues, builds quality links and tracks rankings and traffic using tools like Google Search Console and Analytics. Q. What does a lead generation executive do? A. The role involves finding and contacting potential customers, qualifying their interest and passing suitable leads to the sales team, typically using email, calls, social platforms and CRM tools. Q. How do I apply for a job opening online? A. Read the role description, update your resume to match the required skills, attach your portfolio or GitHub link if relevant, and submit the application through the listed form or email. Q. What happens after I submit a job application? A. Applications are usually screened first, followed by a skills assessment or technical round, then an HR or managerial interview, and finally an offer. Steps and timelines vary by company. Q. What is an internship and how is it different from training? A. An internship places you inside a working team on real tasks, while training is usually classroom-style learning of skills. Internships focus on applying skills in a professional setting. Q. How long does a typical web development internship last? A. Most internships run between 3 and 6 months, though shorter and longer programmes exist depending on the company and the learning goals. Q. What skills help me get a web development internship? A. Basics of HTML, CSS and JavaScript, one or two small projects, familiarity with Git and a willingness to learn are usually enough for entry-level internships. Q. Can I do a digital marketing or SEO internship without experience? A. Yes. Many internships are designed for beginners. Understanding basic concepts like keywords, social media and Google Analytics, and having a sample project or blog, gives you an advantage. Q. Can an internship lead to a full-time job? A. It can. Many companies hire strong interns after the programme, though it depends on performance and open positions, and it is not guaranteed. Q. What should I include in an internship application? A. Send an updated resume, a short note on why you want the internship, your academic or personal projects and links to your GitHub, portfolio or LinkedIn profile. Q. What makes a website design good? A. A good design has clear navigation, readable text, consistent branding, fast loading, a mobile-friendly layout and obvious calls to action so visitors can find what they need quickly. Q. What is responsive web design? A. Responsive design makes a website adjust its layout automatically to fit any screen size, from phones and tablets to desktops, using flexible grids and images. Q. What is the difference between UI and UX design? A. UI is the visual look of the interface, such as buttons, colours and typography. UX is the overall experience and how easily a person can complete tasks on the site. Q. How many design revisions are normal in a website project? A. Two to three rounds of revisions per stage is common. The exact number should be written in your agreement so both sides know what is included. Q. When should a business redesign its website? A. Consider a redesign if the site looks outdated, loads slowly, is hard to use on mobile, has a high bounce rate, or no longer reflects your business and offerings. Q. Does web design affect SEO? A. Yes. Page speed, mobile usability, clean structure, heading hierarchy and image optimisation are design decisions that directly influence search performance. Q. What is the difference between a static and a dynamic website? A. A static website shows the same fixed content to every visitor. A dynamic website generates content from a database or user input, such as logins, dashboards or product listings. Q. Should I choose a CMS like WordPress or a custom-built website? A. A CMS is quicker and easier for content-focused sites. A custom build offers more control over features, performance and security, and suits unique or complex requirements. Q. What is the difference between front-end and back-end development? A. Front-end is everything users see and interact with in the browser. Back-end is the server, database and logic that work behind the scenes. Q. What are domain and hosting, and do I need both? A. A domain is your website's address, and hosting is the server space where your site files are stored. You need both for a website to be accessible online. Q. How do I make my website secure? A. Use HTTPS with an SSL certificate, keep software and plugins updated, use strong passwords and two-factor login, validate user input and take regular backups. Q. Does a website need maintenance after launch? A. Yes. Regular updates, security patches, backups, performance checks and content changes keep a website safe, fast and working properly over time. Q. What is a web portal and how is it different from a website? A. A web portal is a login-based platform that gives different users personalised access to information and tools. A regular website is mostly public content open to all visitors. Q. What are the common types of web portals? A. Common types include customer portals, employee portals, vendor or supplier portals, B2B and B2C portals, student or education portals and community platforms. Q. What features does a typical portal include? A. Typical features are user registration and login, role-based access, dashboards, document uploads, notifications, search, reports and an admin panel. Q. What is role-based access control? A. Role-based access control gives each user permissions based on their role, such as admin, manager or customer, so people only see and do what they are allowed to. Q. Can a portal integrate with existing ERP or CRM systems? A. Yes, usually through APIs. Integration depends on whether the existing system offers an API and on the data you want to exchange. Q. How is user data kept secure in a portal? A. Common practices include encrypted connections, hashed passwords, access controls, activity logs, regular backups and compliance with applicable data protection laws such as India's DPDP Act, 2023. Q. Which platform is better for an online store: Shopify, WooCommerce or custom? A. Shopify is quick to launch and fully hosted, WooCommerce gives flexibility on WordPress, and a custom build suits unusual workflows or large-scale needs. The right choice depends on budget, catalogue size and required features. Q. Which payment gateways can an Indian online store use? A. Popular options include Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree, CCAvenue and PhonePe Payment Gateway, supporting UPI, cards, net banking and wallets. Check each gateway's fees and settlement time. Q. Can an online store save customers' card details? A. Under RBI rules, merchants and gateways cannot store actual card numbers, and saved-card payments use tokenisation instead. Using an authorised payment gateway keeps card handling off your own server. Q. Do I need GST registration to sell online? A. It depends on your turnover, the type of goods or services and whether you sell through marketplaces. Check the current rules with a chartered accountant before launching. Q. What legal pages should an e-commerce website have? A. Include a privacy policy, terms and conditions, return and refund policy, shipping policy and contact details. India's Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 also require seller and grievance information to be shown. Q. What features matter most for an online store? A. Fast product pages, clear search and filters, a simple checkout, multiple payment options, inventory tracking, order notifications and mobile-friendly design have the biggest impact on completed purchases. Q. What is the difference between custom software and off-the-shelf software? A. Off-the-shelf software is built for many users and is ready to use. Custom software is built around your specific processes, so it fits exactly but takes longer and costs more upfront. Q. What are the stages of the software development life cycle? A. The usual stages are requirement analysis, planning, design, development, testing, deployment and maintenance. Q. What is an MVP in software development? A. An MVP, or minimum viable product, is a first version with only the core features, released early to test the idea with real users before investing in the full product. Q. What is SaaS? A. SaaS, or software as a service, is software hosted in the cloud and accessed through a browser, usually on a subscription basis, with no installation required. Q. What is the difference between Agile and Waterfall? A. Waterfall completes each phase in sequence, while Agile delivers work in short cycles with regular feedback. Agile suits projects where requirements may change. Q. How long does it take to develop custom software? A. A simple internal tool can take a few weeks, while a complex business platform can take several months. Scope, integrations and how quickly decisions are made all affect the timeline. Q. What is the difference between data science, data analytics and business intelligence? A. Business intelligence reports on what happened using dashboards. Data analytics explains why it happened. Data science goes further with statistical models and machine learning to predict what may happen next. Q. What is predictive analytics? A. Predictive analytics uses historical data and statistical or machine learning models to estimate future outcomes, such as sales demand, customer churn or equipment failure. Q. What data do I need to start a data science project? A. You need relevant, reasonably clean historical data connected to the question you want answered. Quality and volume matter, and data cleaning is often the biggest part of the work. Q. Can small businesses benefit from data analytics? A. Yes. Even basic analysis of sales, customer and website data can reveal trends, best-selling products and cost-saving opportunities without large budgets. Q. What is the difference between AI and machine learning? A. AI is the broad field of making machines perform tasks that need human-like intelligence. Machine learning is a subset where systems learn patterns from data instead of following fixed rules. Q. Which tools are commonly used for data analysis? A. Common tools include Python, R and SQL for analysis, and Power BI or Tableau for dashboards and visual reporting. Q. What is the difference between native and cross-platform app development? A. Native apps are built separately for each platform, using Kotlin for Android and Swift for iOS. Cross-platform apps use a single codebase, with tools like Flutter or React Native, to run on both. Q. Should I build for Android or iOS first? A. Check where your target users are. Android has the larger share of users in India, while iOS is stronger in some premium segments. Cross-platform development lets you cover both together. Q. How long does it take to develop a mobile app? A. A simple app can take 2 to 3 months, while apps with payments, real-time features or complex back ends often take 4 to 8 months or more. Q. What does it cost to publish an app on the Play Store and App Store? A. Google Play charges a one-time registration fee of 25 US dollars, and the Apple Developer Program costs 99 US dollars per year. Q. How long does app store approval take? A. Review times vary. Apple often reviews within a couple of days and Google Play can take from a few hours to several days, longer for new developer accounts or policy issues. Q. What is the difference between a mobile app and a mobile-friendly website? A. A mobile-friendly website runs in the browser, while an app is installed on the device and can use features such as push notifications, camera and offline access. Q. What is the difference between SEO and SEM? A. SEO improves your unpaid, organic rankings in search results. SEM usually refers to paid search advertising, such as Google Ads, where you pay for visibility. Q. How long does SEO take to show results? A. Most websites see meaningful movement in 3 to 6 months, and competitive keywords can take longer. Results depend on competition, site quality and consistent effort. Q. Can anyone guarantee a number one ranking on Google? A. No. Google's rankings depend on many factors outside any agency's control, so guaranteed rankings are a warning sign. Q. What is local SEO and why does it matter? A. Local SEO helps a business appear in searches tied to a location, such as near me queries and Google Maps. A complete Google Business Profile, consistent contact details and reviews are key factors. Q. How does Google Ads pricing work? A. Google Ads works on an auction. In pay-per-click campaigns you pay when someone clicks your ad, and the cost depends on keyword competition, your bid and your ad quality. Q. What are on-page, off-page and technical SEO? A. On-page SEO covers content and page elements, off-page SEO covers external signals such as backlinks, and technical SEO covers site speed, crawlability, indexing and structured data. Q. What is DLT registration for bulk SMS in India? A. Under TRAI regulations, businesses must register on a telecom operator's DLT platform with their entity details, sender ID (header) and message templates before sending commercial SMS. Q. What is the difference between transactional and promotional SMS? A. Transactional SMS carries alerts and information such as OTPs and order updates. Promotional SMS carries marketing offers and is restricted to customers who have not opted out. Q. Can promotional SMS be sent to DND numbers? A. Promotional SMS cannot be sent to numbers registered on the Do Not Disturb list unless the recipient has given consent. Transactional messages are not blocked by DND. Q. At what times can promotional SMS be sent? A. TRAI rules allow promotional commercial messages only between 9 AM and 9 PM. Q. How many characters fit in one SMS? A. A single SMS holds 160 characters in standard text and 70 characters when it contains Unicode such as regional languages. Longer messages are split into multiple parts and billed per part. Q. How does an SMS API work? A. An SMS API lets your website or app send messages automatically by calling the provider's gateway with the recipient number and a registered template, and it returns delivery status. Q. What should a doctor or clinic website include? A. Include doctor profiles and qualifications, services offered, clinic address and map, timings, contact details, appointment booking, patient information and a privacy policy. Q. Does HIPAA apply to websites in India? A. HIPAA is a United States law and applies to US healthcare entities. In India, patient data handling is governed mainly by the Information Technology Act and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Q. Is online appointment booking safe for patient data? A. It can be when the system uses encrypted connections, limits the data collected, restricts staff access and stores records securely with backups. Q. Are there advertising restrictions for doctors online? A. Yes. Medical professionals in India are bound by the Professional Conduct Regulations, which limit advertising and solicitation. Websites should stick to factual information and avoid exaggerated claims. Q. What are the rules for telemedicine in India? A. Telemedicine is governed by the Telemedicine Practice Guidelines, 2020, which require registered medical practitioners and set rules for consent, records and prescriptions. Q. How can patients find my clinic on Google Maps? A. Claim a Google Business Profile, choose the correct medical specialty category, and add accurate address, phone, hours, services and an appointment link that match your website. Respond to reviews politely without disclosing any patient information. Q. How does blogging help SEO? A. Blog posts let a website target more keywords and answer user questions, which can bring more organic traffic. They also give other sites something to link to and keep the site fresh. Q. How often should a business publish blog posts? A. Consistency matters more than frequency. Publishing quality posts once or twice a month on a regular schedule works better than many rushed posts. Q. How long should a blog post be? A. There is no fixed length. A post should be long enough to fully answer the question. Many informational posts fall between 800 and 2,000 words. Q. What topics should a business blog cover? A. Cover questions customers actually ask, how-to guides, industry updates and common problems. Search Console queries and customer emails are good sources of ideas. Q. What is evergreen content? A. Evergreen content stays relevant over a long time, such as how-to guides and explainers, unlike news posts which become outdated quickly. Q. How do I make a blog post easier to rank? A. Match the search intent, use a clear title and headings, answer the question early, add internal links, optimise images and make sure the page loads quickly on mobile.
