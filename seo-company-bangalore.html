AI Search Optimisation: GEO and AEO for Bangalore Businesses

People now get answers from Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity before they click a single link. Businesses that explain themselves clearly are the ones these tools cite.

What is AEO?

Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO) is the practice of writing and structuring content so search engines and voice assistants can lift a short, direct answer from your page.

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) is the practice of making your brand and content easy for AI tools such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to understand, trust and cite.

Does traditional SEO still matter?

Yes. AI tools draw on pages that already rank, load fast and show clear expertise, so strong technical and content SEO is the base for AI visibility.

How we help you get cited

  • Write direct, quotable answers near the top of important pages
  • Add Organization, LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage and BreadcrumbList schema
  • Show real authors, team details, case studies and numbers that prove experience
  • Keep your name, address and phone consistent across the web
  • Track AI Overview and AI Mode impressions in Search Console and check AI answers manually each month

Frequently Asked Questions About SEO in Bangalore

Straight answers to the questions we hear most from Bangalore business owners.

How do you measure the success of SEO campaigns?

We measure SEO by business outcomes, not just rankings. Each month we track keyword positions, impressions, clicks, organic traffic, referring domains and, most importantly, leads from form submissions, phone clicks and WhatsApp clicks. These numbers are tracked in Google Search Console and GA4 and shared in a monthly report.

Is SEO a one-time activity or an ongoing process?

SEO is an ongoing process. Setup work such as fixing technical issues and optimising key pages is done first, but search results, competitors and Google's algorithms keep changing. Regular content updates, link building and monthly reviews are needed to gain and hold rankings.

Can SEO help generate leads and enquiries, not just traffic?

Yes. SEO targets keywords that people use when they are ready to hire or buy, so the traffic is high-intent. We pair this with clear calls to action, fast mobile pages and conversion tracking so you can see how many enquiries, calls and WhatsApp messages come from search.

Do you provide SEO services only for Bangalore businesses?

No. We are based in Jayanagar, Bangalore and are strongest in local SEO for Bangalore businesses, but we also work with companies elsewhere in India and abroad that need national or international SEO.

What is the difference between local SEO and national SEO?

Local SEO helps a business appear for searches tied to a place, such as "SEO company near me" or "in Bangalore", and in Google Maps results. It relies on a Google Business Profile, consistent name, address and phone details, local citations and reviews. National SEO targets searches without a location and depends more on content depth, authority and backlinks.

Is local SEO important for Bangalore-based businesses?

Yes. Many Bangalore searches have local intent, and Google often shows a map pack above regular results. A complete Google Business Profile, genuine reviews and consistent local details help nearby customers find and call you.

How long does SEO take to show results in Bangalore?

Most websites see meaningful movement in 3 to 6 months, and competitive keywords can take longer. Results depend on competition, the current state of your website, content quality and consistent effort. Nobody can honestly guarantee a number one ranking.

SEO cost depends on competition, the size of your website, the number of locations and keywords, and how much content and link building is needed.
